Dung file nay truoc khi tao mot game moi, hoac truoc khi giao giao vien mot kịch bản day hoc bang game. Muc tieu la de game nao cung co chung mot bo thong tin van hanh.

## 2. Muc tieu su pham

- Game nay dung de lam gi:
  - Warm-up
  - Checkpoint
  - On thi
  - Debrief
  - Lam viec nhom
  - Tong ket chu de
- Sau game, giao vien muon nhin thay dieu gi o hoc sinh?
- Chi so thanh cong cua tiet hoc la gi?

## 3. Chon engine

- Engine du kien:
  - Classic live
  - Dưa toc do
  - Rung chuong vang
  - Ai la trieu phu
  - Presentation
  - Blueprint / chua co engine rieng
- Tai sao chon engine nay thay vi engine khac?

## 4. Cau truc noi dung
