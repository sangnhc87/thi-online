5. Mau quy trinh de them mot game moi ma khong bi vo he thong

01

Chot muc tieu su pham

Game moi phai tra loi duoc: no giup warm-up, checkpoint, on thi, debrief hay project-based learning?

02

Quyet dinh dung engine cu hay can engine moi

Neu game chi thay pacing, scoring va flow, uu tien build tren liveRooms hien co. Chi tao engine moi khi logic thuc su khac hang.

03

Chot input format va starter pack

Moi game can co chuan go, file mau, vi du import-ready va HDSD rieng. Neu khong, giao vien se khen concept nhung khong dung duoc.

04

Chot man truoc tran va sau tran

Ready check, lobby, reveal, leaderboard, award stage, debrief la mot phan cua san pham, khong chi la trang tri.

Ket luan thuc dung

Neu uu tien la ra nhieu game nhanh ma van chac, huong di dung la: giu 5 engine hien co lam loi, them playbook + template + stage flow cho tung nhom muc tieu, sau do moi tach ra thanh engine moi khi mot format da duoc dung lap di lap lai va co gia tri rieng.

6. Checklist truoc khi giao mot game moi cho giao vien

  1. Co file mau import-ready hoac playbook ro rang.
  2. Co quy tac soan cau hoi va pacing goi y.
  3. Co giai thich nen dung cho muc tieu day hoc nao.
  4. Co lobby, ready, reveal, ranking hoac debrief phu hop.
  5. Co man tong ket sau tran de giao vien ket thuc tiet hoc gon.
